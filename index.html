How scoring works

Complexity points = 100 × difficulty tier + 5 × shortest-path steps + 10 × junctions on that path.

Speed bonus = complexity points × 60 ÷ (60 + active seconds), rounded down. Faster completion earns more.

Pauses do not count as play time. Restarts keep time already spent. Each maze can score only once per day.

Your daily total adds all three maze scores. Progress and daily history are saved on this browser/device.

    YOUR FIELD GUIDE

    A little turn.
    A new perspective.

    Guide Clover through the paths to the carrot gate. You can use the arrow keys, WASD, or tap and hold the on-screen arrows.

    1. Up belongs to the maze. The coral compass arrow always shows where the ↑ key will move Clover.
    2. The maze turns. The buttons stay put. The maze and bunny rotate 90° at random intervals. The arrow pad stays fixed like your keyboard. Each key still moves Clover in the maze’s original direction.
    3. Take your time. There’s no time limit. Pause whenever you like, or ask for a hint. Pauses stop the clock; restarts keep time already spent.
    4. Three mazes, one daily score. Complete today’s three mazes in order. Complexity earns base points; faster active completion adds a speed bonus. Each maze counts once. A new set arrives tomorrow.
