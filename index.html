YOUR FIELD GUIDE

A little turn.
A new perspective.

Guide Clover through the paths to the carrot gate. You can use the arrow keys, WASD, or tap and hold the on-screen arrows.

  1. Up belongs to the maze. The coral compass arrow always shows where the ↑ key will move Clover.
  2. The maze turns. The buttons stay put. The maze and bunny rotate 90° at random intervals. The arrow pad stays fixed like your keyboard. Each key still moves Clover in the maze’s original direction.
  3. Take your time. There’s no time limit. Pause whenever you like, or ask for a hint.
